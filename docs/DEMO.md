# Demo script — a 2-minute tour of LoopFeed

Start with `npm run dev` and open http://localhost:5173.

## 1. Feed (30s)

- The feed shows posts from people you follow, newest first. Every image is
  generated art — each person has their own style (Mara = sunset, Theo = neon).
- **Double-click a post image** — the heart bursts and the like sticks.
- Tap the bookmark to save; the post appears under **Profile → Saved**.
- Open the comments, add one, press Enter.

## 2. Loops (20s)

- Mara's top post has a **12/30 progress ring**: “30 days of clay”.
- Go to **Explore**, tick **Loops only** — the grid filters to series posts.

## 3. Story polls (30s)

- Tap Theo's story circle in the tray (it has a 📊 badge). His story asks
  “Which palette should I ship for day 42?” — vote **Neon grid** and watch the
  live percentages appear.
- Stories auto-advance after 6s; use ←/→ or the Prev/Next buttons; Esc closes.
- Noor's story is gone — it expired (stories live 24h).

## 4. Search + profile (20s)

- Press **/**, type `one-pot` — Ravi's Loop post shows up under Posts.
- Open Mara's profile: stats, bio, posts grid. Hit **Follow** / **Following**
  and watch the follower count move. **Message** jumps to a DM thread.

## 5. DMs + notifications (20s)

- **Messages** has an unread badge (Mara's “it fires brown” reply). Open the
  thread — the badge clears. Send a reply; the read ticks show ✓ then ✓✓.
- **Notifications** lists the like/comment/follow/Loop updates. **Mark all read**
  clears the badge.

## 6. Create + reset (20s)

- **Create**: pick an artwork style, hit 🎲 for new sample art (or upload your
  own image), tick “part of a Loop”, name the series, share — your post lands
  at the top of the feed with its own progress ring.
- Toggle **dark mode** in the top bar. When you're done, **Reset demo** restores
  the original seed.
